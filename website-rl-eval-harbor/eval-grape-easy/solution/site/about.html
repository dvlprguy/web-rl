03 — THE MAKER

On Clay and Patience

I came to clay late, after years of work that left no mark on the world. The wheel asked for something I had been missing — attention, and the willingness to begin again. Every vessel here is shaped, dried, and fired in a small studio at the edge of town, one batch at a time.

THE PROCESS

The clay is centred on the wheel and opened by hand.

It rests, dries slowly, and is trimmed to its final line.

Glazed, then fired hot — earth made permanent.

We dig and reclaim our clay within a day's drive of the studio. The colour of each glaze is the colour of the ground it came from.

Mara Ellery

Maker, and keeper of the kiln.
