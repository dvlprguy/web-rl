05 — BESPOKE & TRADE

Some tables ask for something made only for them.

BESPOKE

Commissions

We take on a small number of custom commissions each season — a service for a restaurant, a wedding gift, a single statement piece. We begin with a conversation about how the work will be used, then shape a sample for your approval before the full run is made.

Lead times run six to ten weeks depending on scale. Every commission is glazed in tones drawn from our house palette.

TRADE

Wholesale

We supply a select group of shops and tables with our standing collection. Minimum first orders begin at twelve pieces; reorders carry no minimum. Trade pricing sits at half of retail, with eight-week lead times on production runs.

We are glad to send physical samples and a full line sheet on request.

To begin a conversation, write to trade@terraandvessel.studio and tell us a little about your table.
