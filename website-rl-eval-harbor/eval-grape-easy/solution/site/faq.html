04 — QUESTIONS

Questions, Answered

A few plain words on care, shipping, and the promise behind every piece.

CARE & CLEANING

How do I care for my piece?

Wash by hand with warm water and a soft cloth — no harsh scouring. Each piece is food-safe and built for daily use; the glaze will deepen and soften with time, which is part of its life. Avoid sudden shifts from freezer to oven.

SHIPPING & TIMELINES

When will my order arrive?

Because we fire to order, most pieces leave the studio within four weeks. We pack each one by hand in recycled wool and paper, and you will receive a note the day it ships. We post worldwide at cost.

RETURNS & GUARANTEE

What if something isn't right?

If a piece arrives damaged, tell us within fourteen days and we will remake or refund it, no fuss. Beyond that, every vessel carries our lifetime care — chips and cracks from honest use can often be mended in the studio.

Still wondering something? Write to us at hello@terraandvessel.studio — we answer every letter ourselves.
