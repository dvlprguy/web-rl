Strategy · Intermediate

Foundations of Strategic Thinking

6 modules · approx. 12 hours · lifetime access

Enrol in this course

The intent

Strategy is too often presented as a dark art. This course removes the mystique. Over six measured modules, you will learn to distinguish a genuine strategy from a mere plan, to identify the few decisions that matter, and to articulate a direction that others can actually follow.

The syllabus

Six modules, in sequence.

  1. 01

    What strategy is — and is not

    Separating direction from activity, and ambition from intent.

  2. 02

    Reading the landscape

    Understanding context, constraint and the moving parts around you.

  3. 03

    The diagnosis

    Naming the central challenge before reaching for solutions.

  4. 04

    The guiding policy

    Choosing an approach, and accepting what it asks you to forgo.

  5. 05

    Coherent action

    Designing moves that reinforce one another rather than scatter.

  6. 06

    Communicating direction

    Making the strategy clear enough that others can carry it.

Your instructor

Edmund Whitlock

Former Head of Strategy, Penhaligon Industries · Visiting Fellow, Carwood Business School

“I spent twenty years watching clever people confuse motion with progress. This course is the conversation I wish someone had had with me at the start.”

What you walk away with

A method you can apply on Monday: the ability to frame any complex situation, isolate the decisions that matter, and communicate a direction with quiet confidence. And a certificate to mark it.
