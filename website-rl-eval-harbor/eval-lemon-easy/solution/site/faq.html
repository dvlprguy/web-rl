QUESTIONS

// 01 What time should I arrive?

Doors open at 22:30. The first set lands at 23:00 sharp. Come early. The room is best when it's still half-dark and half-empty.

// 02 Is there a dress code?

No. Wear something you can move in. We keep the lights low so nobody is looking anyway.

// 03 Where exactly is the venue?

The address is sent by email the night before. We move locations. Watch your inbox.

// 04 Can I bring a camera?

Phones away. No flash. The set lives in the room, not the feed.

// 05 Are tickets refundable?

Up to 48 hours before doors. After that the spot is yours, kept or not.

Still unsure? Talk to us.
