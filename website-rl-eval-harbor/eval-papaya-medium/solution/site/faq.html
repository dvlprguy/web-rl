Booking

How quickly is a reservation confirmed?

Most stays confirm instantly. A small number of curated properties review requests within four hours — we hold your dates while they do.

Can I book on behalf of someone else?

Yes. Simply note the lead guest during checkout, and we'll address all correspondence accordingly.

Payments

Which payment methods do you accept?

All major cards and most regional wallets. Charges appear as “Lumen & Latitude” on your statement.

When am I charged?

For instant bookings, at confirmation. For request stays, only once the host accepts.

Cancellation

What is the standard cancellation window?

Most stays offer a full refund up to 72 hours before arrival. Each listing states its exact policy plainly before you commit.

Safety

How do you verify properties?

Every stay passes a 40-point review and a verified-host check before earning its glowing badge.
