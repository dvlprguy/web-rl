How it works

  1. 01

    Deposit your seed

    Place a minimum of $10,000 into the account. A single lump or a measured opening — either takes root the same day.

  2. 02

    Choose your season

    Commit to a five-year term. We mark the harvest date on your ledger and send you nothing but quiet annual statements until then.

  3. 03

    Let it compound

    Interest is credited daily and reinvested without instruction. You need do nothing — patience is the only labor required.

  4. 04

    Reap or replant

    At maturity, withdraw the whole or roll it into a fresh term at the prevailing rate. The choice, as always, is yours.

Questions, plainly answered

Can I withdraw early?
Yes, with thirty days' notice. An early lift forfeits accrued interest above the savings rate — a gentle nudge toward patience, never a trap.
Is my deposit insured?
Fully, to the regulatory ceiling, and reserves are audited twice yearly by an independent house.
Does the rate change mid-term?
Never. The yield you open with is the yield you keep for all five years — fixed in writing, fixed in soil.
Are there hidden fees?
None. No opening fee, no maintenance fee, no statement fee. If a charge cannot be explained in one sentence, we do not levy it.
