Our Ground

A bank that thinks in seasons.

Loam & Ledger began in 1908, when a miller and a notary pooled their savings to lend patiently to neighbors the larger houses ignored. They wrote one rule into the charter and never amended it: lend as you would tend land — slowly, honestly, and with the next generation in mind.

Over a century later we manage considerably more, but the rule is unchanged. We do not trade for the thrill of it. We do not chase the loudest yield. We compound quietly, and we are still here.

A patient chronology

  • 1908

    The first ledger

    A miller and a notary open a single lending book above the grain store.

  • 1934

    We hold steady

    Through the lean years, not a single steward's loan is called in haste.

  • 1962

    North Furrow acquired

    The house buys its first vineyard — capital made tangible in soil.

  • 1991

    The Growth Account

    Our patient five-year instrument is introduced and never re-priced retroactively.

  • 2024

    Still tending

    Fourth-generation clients, the same charter, the same long horizon.

What we hold to

Patience over haste.

The market rewards those who can simply wait. We make waiting comfortable.

Plainness over polish.

Clear statements, honest fees, and counsel given face to face.

Stewardship over scale.

We grow only as fast as we can grow well. Trust does not scale by force.
