Shipping

01 How are objects packed?
Each piece is crated by hand in untreated wood and wool. Glass and stone travel separately, never stacked.
02 Do you ship internationally?
We ship worldwide. Heavier pieces are sent by freight; a courier confirms a window with you directly.

Care

03 How should stoneware be cleaned?
Warm water and a soft cloth. No solvents. The glaze deepens with use — this is intended.
04 Will bronze tarnish?
It will patinate. We consider the change part of the object's life and do not recommend polishing.

Returns

05 May I return a piece?
Standard editions may be returned within fourteen days, unused. Limited and bespoke works are final.

Commissions

06 Can you make to order?
Yes. Bespoke work begins with a letter. We respond within the week and quote in stages.
07 How long does a commission take?
Between three and nine months. We do not hurry the material.

A quiet letter, four times a year. Materials, makers, and the slow work of the atelier.
