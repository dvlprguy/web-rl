I.

The House

Maison Solenne was founded on a single conviction: that an object should hold its ground. We began in a narrow workshop off the Rue Volney, with one kiln and a borrowed lathe.

We have never grown quickly. Each season brings a handful of pieces, drawn from materials we trust and makers we know by name. Nothing leaves the atelier without rest.
