Can I change plans whenever?

Anytime, in a tap. Upgrades start at once; downgrades take effect at your next cycle. No friction, no phone calls.

Is there a contract?

None. Membership is month to month and cancels in a single click. We'd rather you stay because you like it.

Do membership fees go toward stays?

Your savings on member rates usually cover the fee within a single trip — so it tends to pay for itself.

A soft promise.

If your first stay isn't a place you'd happily return to, we'll refund it in full — no awkward conversation required.
