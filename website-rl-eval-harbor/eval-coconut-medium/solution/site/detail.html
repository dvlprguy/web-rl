Hillcrest Hush

A one-room cottage · Tucked in the green folds of Mossgill Vale

Hillcrest Hush is exactly what its name promises: a single warm room at the top of a slope, where the loudest thing is the kettle and the view goes on for soft green miles. There's a deep bed by the window, a chair that's seen a thousand good books, and a wood stove that takes the edge off any evening.

We left you a note on the sill and a tin of biscuits in the cupboard. Beyond that, the day is entirely yours — wander the lane, nap until tea, or simply watch the hills change color as the light goes.

  • Hot rainfall shower
  • Hill-view porch with two chairs
  • Handwritten welcome note on arrival
