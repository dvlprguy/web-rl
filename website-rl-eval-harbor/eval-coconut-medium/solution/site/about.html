Chapter One

The story behind the quill

It started with a borrowed cottage and a notebook left on the sill. We came for a long weekend and stayed a little longer, because the place asked nothing of us. There was a kettle, a quilt, and a handwritten note from the owner that began, "Make yourself entirely at home."

That note became the whole idea. Marshmallow & Quill collects the kind of stays that feel personally addressed to you — soft, unhurried, a touch whimsical. We visit every one, write the description by hand, and leave a real note behind. Comfort, we think, is a craft.

* The original note is framed in our office, slightly tea-stained.

† We still use fountain pens. Refills are a hazard.

Our little timeline

  1. 2019 — The borrowed cottage. A weekend that quietly changed our minds about travel.
  2. 2020 — First five stays. Visited, photographed, and written up by hand.
  3. 2022 — The note program. Every guest now arrives to a handwritten welcome.
  4. Today — Forty soft places. Still small, still earnest, still putting the kettle on.

What we hold dear

Genuine comfort

Not staged, not styled to death. Beds you sink into and chairs you forget the time in.

Handpicked honesty

We've slept in every stay. If it isn't a soft place to land, it doesn't make the list.
