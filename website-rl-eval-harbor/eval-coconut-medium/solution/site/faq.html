How do I book a stay?

Find a place you love, tell us your dates, and tap the rounded reserve button. We confirm within a day and start writing your welcome note.

Have you actually visited each stay?

Every single one. If we haven't slept in it and loved it, it doesn't make the list — that's the whole point of handpicked.

When am I charged?

A small deposit holds your dates; the balance is due a week before arrival. No surprise fees hiding in the fine print.

What if I need to cancel?

Plans soften and change — we understand. Cancel up to 14 days before and your deposit moves to a future stay, no fuss.

Is the handwritten note really handwritten?

It truly is. A real person, a real pen, slightly imperfect cursive. It's our favorite part.

Still puzzled?

Pour yourself a cup and write to us. A host will reply with the answer — and, probably, a kind word.
