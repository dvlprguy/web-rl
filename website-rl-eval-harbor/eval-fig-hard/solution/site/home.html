04 Doctrine

On the discipline of the body.

We hold that progress is not a feeling but a record. Each member is a longitudinal study of their own physiology — charted across load, recovery, and time. The Society exists to remove guesswork.

There are no mirrors of vanity here, only instruments. The body is mapped, the work is logged, and the certification follows.
