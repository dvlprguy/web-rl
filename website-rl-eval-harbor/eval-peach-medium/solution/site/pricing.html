Questions
Can I pay across the term?

Yes — fees can be split across the weeks of a course at no extra cost. Ask the studio manager at enrollment.

Do I need experience?

No. Foundation tracks assume nothing. We start at the material and build from there.

What's in the materials kit?

Everything the course needs — tools, samples, and a working ledger — kept yours to take home.

Can I change tracks mid-season?

Season Members and Residents can move between tracks as the calendar allows.
