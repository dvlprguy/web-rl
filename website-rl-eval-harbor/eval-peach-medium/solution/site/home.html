01 / Why slow craft

Material first

We start with the substance in hand — its weight, grain, and stubbornness — and let the lesson follow from it.

Notebook habit

Every session is recorded in a working ledger. Progress is something you can turn back to and read again.

Quiet repetition

Skill arrives through repetition without rush. We make room for the unhurried hour.
