Featured maker

Priya Ansel

Priya came to the school from a textile workshop where she had spent a decade chasing colour that would not fade. Her teaching begins at the dye pot and ends in the ledger — every shade weighed, timed, and noted so it can be made again.

She leads Roots & Rust and keeps the school's library of pigment samples, a wall of small swatches that grows by one each week.
