Overview

The Earthwork Series begins below the surface. Over eight weeks you learn to read a soil profile the way a binder reads a page — by its layers, its color, and what it has held before you arrived.

We move slowly and on purpose: each lesson asks you to touch, weigh, and record before you act. By the close of the course you will keep a bed of your own and a ledger that explains every choice made within it.

“Good ground is not found. It is kept, season by season, by someone willing to write it down.”
