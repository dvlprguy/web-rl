No. Checking and High-Yield Savings carry no monthly fee, no minimum balance, and no overdraft penalties. The full fee list fits on a single screen — see our Pricing page.

Most applications take under ten minutes. You'll need a government ID and a funding source. Approval is usually immediate.

Yes. High-Yield Savings has no withdrawal penalties or lockups. Vault CDs are the exception — those pay out at maturity.
