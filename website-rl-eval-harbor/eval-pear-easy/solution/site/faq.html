FAQ

Reasonable questions, answered with the same economy we ask of our writers.

Q1 — publishing

How often do you publish?

One lead piece each week, occasionally a short note between. We would rather miss a deadline than miss the point.

Q2 — pitching

Can I pitch a piece?

Yes. Send one paragraph describing the idea and why it matters now. If it holds together at that length, we will read further.

Q3 — access

Is there a paywall?

The lead story is always free to read. Members fund the archive and receive the weekly note. No advertising, ever.
