Giving

Where does my money actually go?

Directly into meals, fuel, and stipends. Last year 88¢ of every dollar reached a program. See the breakdown on our transparency page.

Can I give monthly?

Yes, and it's our favorite way — steady gifts let us plan routes weeks ahead. Set it up on the Give page.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

In most regions, yes. You'll receive a receipt by email within minutes and a yearly summary each January.

Can I give to one program only?

Absolutely. Each program page has its own giving form — like the one on Warm Plates.

Volunteering

Do I need experience to cook?

None at all. Most volunteers start by chopping vegetables and learn the rest at the table beside someone who's done it a while.

What's the smallest commitment?

One shift. Come once, see how it feels. Many stay; nobody is pressured to.

Can I drive a route?

Yes — you need a valid license and a clean record check. We pair new drivers with a regular for the first two weeks.

Are there roles that aren't food?

Plenty: scheduling, translation, sorting, repairs. Tell us your skill on the contact page.

Transparency

How are you funded?

Roughly 60% individual donors, 25% grants, 15% corporate partners. No single funder exceeds 8% of our budget.

Who governs the collective?

A nine-member board of neighbors, cooks, and finance volunteers. Minutes are posted quarterly.

Can I see your financials?

Yes — our annual report and audited statements live on the About page.

How do you measure impact?

Meals delivered, on-time rate, and repeat households — not vanity numbers. See the outcomes dashboard.

Still stuck?

If your question isn't here, write to us. A real person answers within a day, usually faster.
