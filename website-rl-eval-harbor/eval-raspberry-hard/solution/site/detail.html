Warm Plates Initiative Active

The idea, plainly

Some nights there just isn't a meal. Warm Plates fills that gap with food a neighbor cooked the same day — handed over warm, with no forms and no waiting line.

Volunteers cook in shared kitchens each afternoon. Drivers fan out at dusk along fixed routes. By the time the streetlights flicker on, dinner is on the table for hundreds of households who needed exactly one good evening.

It is the simplest thing we do, and the most loved. Pull up a chair.

Milestones

  • 2019 — First Tuesday cook with 12 volunteers and one borrowed van.
  • 2020 — Scaled to daily routes through the hardest winter on record.
  • 2022 — Crossed 100,000 meals; added the Riverbend kitchen.
  • 2023 — Launched apprentice cooks to lead afternoon shifts.
  • 2024 — 18 routes, citywide, and still no forms at the door.
