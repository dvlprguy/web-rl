Why we gather

“No one should have to earn a warm meal. So we just keep cooking, and the table keeps getting longer.”

How it started

Sunfold began with a too-big pot of soup and a winter that wouldn't quit. One kitchen, a handful of neighbors, and a stubborn idea: that warmth multiplies when you give it away.

Word moved the way it always does — kitchen to doorstep to group chat. Within a season we'd outgrown the stove. Within a year we'd outgrown the building. We never set out to build an organization; we just kept saying yes to the next small thing.

  • One pot became a weekly Big Cook.
  • One street became eighteen routes.
  • One winter became a year-round network.

Hands, a bowl, and a sun — the whole job, really.
