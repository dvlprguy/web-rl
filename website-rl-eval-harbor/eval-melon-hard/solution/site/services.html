Survey & Advisory

Land does not lie, but it does not volunteer either. Our work is to ask it the right questions — boundary, slope, water, yield — and return answers precise enough to sign your name beside.

Process FLOW / 04

  1. 01
    Intake

    We log your parcel into the registry and pull every adjacent record.

  2. 02
    Field Pass

    A surveyor walks the boundary and records contour, drainage, and access.

  3. 03
    Render

    Data becomes plat maps, charts, and a written valuation with confidence ranges.

  4. 04
    Advise

    We meet, read the numbers together, and set a course of action.

Methodology MTHD / 06

Contour-Weighted Value

Slope and aspect adjust raw acreage into usable land. We weight each contour band before pricing.

Frontage & Access

Road and water frontage are measured directly and priced as a premium against landlocked comps.

FAQ FAQ / 07

  • Q-01
    Do you survey land you don't list?

    Yes. Survey and valuation are offered independently of any listing relationship.

  • Q-02
    How current is your comparables data?

    The registry syncs settlements daily; comps are never older than the last business day.

  • Q-03
    Can valuations be used for lending?

    Our Plat Set tier produces lender-ready documentation with confidence ranges.
