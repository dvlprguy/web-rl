Description DOC / 01

Cedar Bench sits on a wide southern shelf above Lindell Creek, its soils a deep loam grading to gravel at the eastern drop. The bench has been grazed lightly for two generations; fences are sound and the seasonal spring near the centroid has never failed in record.

Access runs off Plat Row along a graded easement, giving 220 metres of road frontage before the land tilts gently toward the water. The structure — a 1974 barn — is serviceable and sits clear of the floodplain. A buyer seeking yield, contour, and reliable water will find all three drawn here in olive and mint.
