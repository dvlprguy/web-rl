Foundational program

Strength Roots

Build a body that holds. Strength Roots is our twelve-week progressive resistance program — barbell, dumbbell, and bodyweight — that grows your foundation slowly and surely. We test where you start, scale every lift to your form, and add load only when you've earned it. No ego, no rush, just steady roots.

Enroll in Strength Roots
LevelAll / Scaled
Duration12 weeks
Sessions3 / week
Class sizeMax 14

Curriculum timeline

Weeks 1–3

Root assessment & patterns

Movement screen, baseline lifts, and grooving the squat, hinge, push, and pull with light load.

Weeks 4–6

Building load

Linear progression on the big lifts, accessory work for joints, and introducing tempo control.

Weeks 7–9

Strength density

Heavier sets, supersets, and unilateral work to even out imbalances and build resilience.

Weeks 10–12

Peak & retest

Personal-best attempts, deload, and a final assessment to measure your new roots.
