The fine print

Contracts & commitments

Monthly plans are month-to-month with no long-term contract. Annual plans bill once yearly at the 18% discount and renew automatically unless cancelled 14 days prior.

Membership freezes

Freeze any plan up to 90 days per year for $5/month to hold your rate and progress. Medical freezes are free with documentation.

Cancellation

Cancel anytime from your member portal. Monthly plans end at the close of your current cycle; annual plans receive a prorated refund minus the discount difference.
