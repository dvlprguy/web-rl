Getting Started

How do I book my first class?

Download the Rootwell app or visit the front desk. Newcomers get a free orientation walk-through with a trainer before their first session.

What should I wear?

Comfortable movement clothes and clean indoor shoes. Lockers, towels, and water stations are all provided.

Do I need experience?

None at all. Every class lists a level, and trainers scale every movement to where you're planted today.

Billing

When am I charged?

Monthly plans bill on your join date each month. Annual plans bill once at signup with the 18% discount applied.

Can I switch tiers?

Yes, anytime from your portal. Upgrades apply immediately; downgrades take effect next cycle.

Do you offer refunds?

Annual plans are prorated on cancellation. Add-ons are refundable within 7 days if unused.

Classes

How big are classes?

Capped at 14 for strength and mobility, 20 for cardio — so coaches always know your name and form.

What if I miss one?

Cancel up to 4 hours before with no penalty. Late cancels use one of three monthly grace passes.

Can I bring kids?

Youth classes run weekend mornings; supervised play care is available during adult sessions.

Facilities

What's in the recovery suite?

Infrared sauna, cold plunge, percussion guns, and a guided mobility corner. Access depends on your tier.

Is there parking?

Free lot parking at all branches, plus covered bike racks and showers for commuters.

Are the studios accessible?

Yes — step-free entry, accessible restrooms, and adaptive equipment at every location.

Still stuck?

Our greenhouse crew answers every message within one business day — often much sooner.
