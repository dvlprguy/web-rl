FAQ
How late can I order?

Until last orders for your zone — the ticker up top always knows the truth.

Can you cater allergies?

Yes. Flag it in the build-your-bowl form and we run a dedicated nut-free table nightly.

Where are my Ladle Points?

Tucked in your account jar. They never expire while you keep ordering.

Do you do refunds?

If a bowl's wrong, we re-send or refund — your call, no quibbles.

Is there a minimum order?

£12 in Z1–Z2, £15 in Z3–Z4. Focaccia counts, obviously.

Can I tip the rider?

Always, in-app or cash. They earn every penny in the rain.
