How the Ladle Swings
"We started with one stockpot, a folding table and a stubborn belief that good soup should never be a luxury. Everything since has been arithmetic and love."
Origin story

2014 Plum & Ladle began as a stall under a dripping awning, selling exactly three soups to a queue that didn't yet know it wanted them. We sold out by one. We did it again the next day, and the next.

2017 Three years and one near-disaster involving 200 litres of misplaced broth later, we opened The Pass — a proper kitchen with a proper spreadsheet habit. We started tracking everything: prep times, sourcing miles, the exact moment a leek goes from charred to ruined.

2020 When the doors had to close, we built delivery from nothing in eleven frantic days. Bowls went out in insulated boxes drawn up on the back of a menu. They arrived hot. People wept a little. So did we.

2024 Today we run four kitchens across four zones, partner with 31 farms within a day's drive, and still taste every recipe at the pass before it goes live. The table got bigger. The stubbornness stayed exactly the same.
