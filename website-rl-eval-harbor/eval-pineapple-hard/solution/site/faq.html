DONATIONS

How do I cancel or change a recurring gift?

Log into your donor dashboard, open "My Giving," and use the pause or edit buttons. Changes apply to the next billing date. No call required.

When will I receive a tax receipt?

One-time gifts get an instant emailed receipt. Recurring donors receive a single consolidated receipt each January covering the prior year.

What percentage actually reaches programs?

87 cents of every dollar. We publish quarterly audits at the Transparency Desk so you can verify the split yourself.

Can I give in honor or memory of someone?

Yes — add a dedication note in the gift form. We'll send an optional card to the person or family you name.

How do I update my payment card?

Under "My Giving," select "Payment method." Updates are encrypted and take effect immediately.
