RECEIPTS & FEES

Will I get a tax receipt?

Yes. Recurring donors receive a consolidated annual receipt every January; one-time gifts get an instant emailed receipt. Both are CRA/IRS-ready.

What fees come out of my donation?

Payment processing runs roughly 2.2% + $0.30 per transaction. We absorb most of this into our 13% operations slice, so 87% still reaches programs.

Can I change or pause my monthly gift?

Anytime, in two clicks from your donor dashboard — no email, no phone call, no guilt.

Is my gift restricted to a program?

By default gifts go to the General Tide Fund, where they flex to greatest need. You can earmark a specific program in the gift form.
